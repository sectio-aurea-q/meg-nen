## d. S065: caption of Table S1, footnotes, ±, number of measurements

- SI page S2 (PDF page 2), caption (three sentences, quoted in parts):
  "Table S1. KIE values for reaction of TaCPa2E with saturating concentrations of CDP-Glc or CDP-[2-2H]Glc in the temperature range of 293.15 - 353.15 K (20-80 °C)."
  "VH: initial reaction rate of CDP-Glc (min-1) and VD: initial reaction rate of CDP-[2-2H]Glc (min-1). The pH was 7.5."
- Column headers: "T (K)" and "KIE (VH VD-1)".
- Footnotes: none. The table has no footnote.
- Meaning of ± / number of measurements: not stated in Table S1 itself or its caption. Stated elsewhere for the same data:
  - Article, Materials and Methods (KIE determination): "Repeated measurements (N ≥ 4) at a single saturating substrate concentration gave kcat values determined with high precision."
  - Same place: "The KIEs (Table S1) were obtained as the ratio of the kcat for enzymatic reaction with the light (1H) and heavy (2H) isotope."
  - Article, caption of Figure 4 (temperature profile of the KIE on kcat): "Symbols show the experimental data, and error bars show the associated S.D. (N = 4)."
  So: S.D., N = 4 (Figure 4 caption) or N ≥ 4 (Methods). That the ± printed in Table S1 is this S.D. is an inference
  from Figure 4 showing the same KIE profile; Table S1 does not say it. How the S.D. of the ratio was formed is not stated.

## e. S065: Table S1 versus Dkcat of article Table 1

| T | SI Table S1 (KIE, VH VD-1) | article Table 1, Dkcat |
|---|---|---|
| 20 °C (293.15 K) | 2.20 ± 0.75 | 2.20 ± 0.75 |
| 60 °C (333.15 K) | 4.29 ± 0.28 | 4.30 ± 0.30 |
| 80 °C (353.15 K) | 3.34 ± 0.09 | 3.34 ± 0.09 |

- 20 and 80 °C: identical, value and error. 60 °C: differs only in the printed digits (4.29 ± 0.28 vs 4.30 ± 0.30),
  consistent with rounding of the same number, but that is my reading, not a statement of the source.
  (Figure S1 caption, SI page S4, and article Results give the 60 °C value as "4.3 ± 0.3 (N = 4)".)
- Does the source say they are different determinations? Yes, by description of the methods, although the numbers do not show it:
  - Table S1 = ratio of kcat from repeated measurements at one saturating concentration (quotes under d).
  - Table 1, footnote a: "The KIEs on kcat (Dkcat) and on kcat/KM (Dkcat/KM) were obtained by a global nonlinear fit to the data for both isotopologue substrates."
  - Article, Materials and Methods: "Note that the KIEs on kcat determined from fits of eq 6 to the data were in excellent agreement (± 5%) with the corresponding KIEs determined from"
    (continues: "(repeated) measurements at a single saturating substrate concentration.")
- Caution for the collection: the source describes two procedures, but the Dkcat printed in Table 1 at 20 and 80 °C is digit
  for digit the Table S1 value including the error, and at 60 °C equals the single-concentration value of Figure S1.
  The printed numbers therefore do not look like independent results; they should not be counted as two independent
  measurements without clarification. The source does not resolve this.
