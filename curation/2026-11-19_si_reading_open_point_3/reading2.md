Labelled positions: not stated beyond the label "D31". Supplier / synthesis: not stated (Materials, section I.a, p. S3, does not mention linoleic acid; I.d only says "following the previous procedure.2", ref. 2 = Meyer, Tomchick, Klinman).

**b) Buffer and pH.** 0.1 M sodium borate, pH 9.0. Location: Table S7 footnotes a and b (p. S46): "protio-LA substrate in 0.1 M sodium borate, pH 9.0". The UV-kinetics paragraph (I.d, p. S3–S4) itself gives no buffer.

**c) Temperatures.** Table S7 (p. S45–S46), "as a function of temperature": WT, S749A, S749G, Y317S, Y317F: 45, 40, 35, 30, 25, 20, 15, 10 °C (8 temperatures). Y317L: 45, 40, 35, 30, 25, 20, 15 °C (7 temperatures; no 10 °C row). Consistent with I.d (p. S4): "from at least seven temperatures for each variant studied here".

**d) How ΔEa and Dkcat were obtained.** Section I.d (p. S3–S4):
- kcat, KM, Dkcat: "determined from non-linear fits in Origin 8"; Table 1 / Figure 5 data are "mean ± s.e.m from three independent measurements".
- Ea(H) and ΔEa: "determined by linear fits of the corresponding Arrhenius plots"; mean ± s.e.m. "from at least seven temperatures".
- Weighting of the fits: not stated.
- Whether ΔEa comes from separate H and D fits or from a fit of ln Dkcat: not stated (only "corresponding Arrhenius plots").
- Temperature at which Dkcat of main-text Table 1 stands: not stated in this SI for Table 1. (Only Table S3, the *previously reported* mutants, has footnote a "Recorded at 30˚C" attached to kcat.) Table S7 gives Dkcat at every temperature, e.g. WT 30 °C: 56.7 (1.5).

Meaning of the bracketed numbers in Table S7: not stated. I.d also: WT parameters in Table 1 "slightly different from the previous reports (Table S3)".

## Q2. S059 SI Appendix (Zaragoza 2023, pnas.2211630120.sapp), I552A and V750A

**a) Heavy substrate.** Perdeuterated linoleic acid, d31-LA. "Enzyme Kinetics" (p. S2–S3): "The perdeuterated linoleic acid (d31-LA) was isolated and purified as previously described (1)" (ref. 1 = Hu, Offenbacher, Lu, Klinman, JBC 2019). Positions beyond "perdeuterated/d31": not stated.

**b) Buffer and pH.** 100 mM borate, pH 9.0. "Enzyme Kinetics" (p. S3): "All assays were performed in 100 mM borate (pH 9.0)". Table S1 footnote a: "Kinetics were performed in 0.1 M borate, pH 9.0."

**c) Temperatures.** Range 10–40 °C: "under an ambient atmosphere at 10 – 40 ˚C" (p. S3). Number: "from at least seven temperatures for each variant reported" (same paragraph). The individual temperatures are not stated. ΔEa: "determined from the difference in activation energies" Ea(H) and Ea(D); Ea(H) by "linear fits to Arrhenius plots". Weighting: not stated.

**d) New or earlier data?** Table S1 (p. S20), column "Reference": I552A and V750A are marked "This work" (all other variants cite Refs. 37, 14, 38, 15). The Methods do not say it in words; only the mutant constructs "were generated as previously described (1)" (Site-Directed Mutagenesis, p. S2). Footnote c of Table S1 gives a contemporaneous WT comparison: "under similar protein and substrate purification procedure, data for WT".

**e) Per-temperature table or figure?** No. The SI has no table or figure with kcat(H), kcat(D) or Dkcat at each temperature for I552A or V750A. Only single-temperature values: Table S1, footnote b "are reported for 30°C": I552A kcat 81 (2) s-1, Dkcat 60 (2), Ea −0.3 (0.2), ΔEa 1.2 (0.5) kcal/mol; V750A kcat 218 (8), Dkcat 69 (4), Ea 1.0 (0.4), ΔEa 0.3 (0.4). Table S4 repeats I552A kcat/Ea ("30 ˚C, 0.1 M borate buffer pH 9.0, Corrected for Fe content"). Figures S1–S12 are HDX, structures, fluorescence, Stokes shift, DSC; none shows Arrhenius plots of kcat. The methods say "A summary of all enzyme kinetics data (kcat at 30 ˚C and Ea)" is in Table 1, S1, S4.

Side note (internal inconsistency): WT kcat is "297 (10)" with Ref. (37) in Table S1 but "297(12)" with "data from Ref (3)" in Table S4.

## Q3. S051 SI (Loveridge 2011, ja208844j_si_001), MpDHFR

The SI contains only tables, figures and 4 references; no methods text. The words "single-turnover" do not occur; Table S10 calls the pH 7 kH data "Pre-steady state kinetic parameters".

**a) pH 7 series.** Table S1 (p. S1), "Temperature dependence of the rate constants for hydride (kH) and deuteride (kD) transfer ... at pH 7": 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 °C (range 5–30 °C).

**b) pH 9 series.** Table S2 (p. S1), same wording "... at pH 9": 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 °C (range 5–30 °C). Note: Table S2 labels the columns kH / kD and does not use the words "steady state"; that these are steady-state (kcat) values is not stated in Table S2 itself (Tables S8/S9 and Figs. S3–S6 speak of "kcat and the KIE on kcat at pH 9").

**c) KIE at the lowest temperature (5 °C).** pH 7 (Table S1): kH 267.6 ± 14.4, kD 118.9 ± 14.5 s-1, KIE 2.25 ± 0.13. pH 9 (Table S2): kH 1.75 ± 0.14, kD 0.67 ± 0.04 s-1, KIE 2.61 ± 0.10.

**d) Temperatures in the Arrhenius fit.** Not stated for MpDHFR. Activation parameters are given without a fit range: pH 9 in Table S3 (EaH 15.6 ± 0.2, EaD 17.1 ± 1.1, ΔEa 1.5 ± 1.1 kJ mol-1, AH/AD 1.38 ± 0.47); pH 7 in Table S14/S18/S22, 0 % column (EaH 21.6 ± 2.2, EaD 28.1 ± 2.6, ΔEa 6.6 ± 3.4, AH/AD 0.13 ± 0.09). The only range remark is Table S3 footnote b "above 15 ºC", attached to EcDHFR entries only, not to MpDHFR.

Side notes (discrepancies inside the SI): (i) pH 9, 20 °C: Table S2 gives 2.46 ± 0.19 s-1 and KIE 2.46 ± 0.13; Table S9 "Buffer alone" gives kcat 3.05 ± 0.12 and KIE(kcat) 2.43 ± 0.09; Table S7 gives 2.46 ± 0.24. (ii) pH 7, 5 °C: Table S1 kH 267.6 ± 14.4 versus Table S6 (MTEN buffered H2O, 5 ºC, pH 7) 195.4 ± 47.1. (iii) Table S3 kH at 25 °C, pH 9 = 2.72 ± 0.21, identical to Table S2.

## Q4. Heyes 2009 excerpt (ref. 12 of S022) and S022 (Heyes 2011, HTML)

**a) 2009 excerpt, Experimental Procedures, "Laser Photoexcitation Measurements".**
- Buffer/pH: 50 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.5, 0.1 % Genapol, 0.1 % β-mercaptoethanol ("containing 50 mm Tris-HCl, pH 7.5, 0.1% Genapol").
- Cryo-solvent: 44 % glycerol and 20 % sucrose ("in 44% glycerol and 20% sucrose"); sample 1 ml with 100 µM POR, 250 µM NADPH (or NADP2H), 30 µM Pchlide. For SIE: D2O, d8-glycerol, deuterated sucrose.
- One solvent over the whole range? Only this one composition is given "For studies of the temperature dependence of rate constants"; no second composition and no change with temperature is mentioned. An explicit statement that it was used at every temperature: not stated. Range named in Results: "wide temperature range (-80 °C to +50 °C)".
- Check of the cryoprotectant: "Control experiments in the absence of any cryoprotectant confirmed" that KIE and SIE "were not influenced by the presence of glycerol or sucrose".
- Caveat: Table 1 of 2009 (25 °C) says "as described under Experimental Procedures"; whether those 25 °C values were taken with or without cryoprotectant is not stated in the excerpt.

Fig. 3A/4A show T. elongatus Eyring plots without attributing the data to ref. 12. The S022 supplemental Tables S1/S2 are not in the sources, so what they say is not stated here.
