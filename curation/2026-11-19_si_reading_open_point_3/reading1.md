## Q1. S057 SI (Offenbacher 2017, oc7b00142_si_001)

**a) Heavy substrate**
- Named only as "D31-LA" (perdeuterated linoleic acid by its label; the word "perdeuterated" is not printed).
  Location: Table S7, footnote b (p. S45). Quote: "Rate constants determined for D31-LA substrate in 0.1 M sodium borate".
- Positions: not stated beyond the label D31. Supplier or synthesis of D31-LA: not stated.
  Materials (I.a, p. S3) say only: "All other reagents were purchased from Sigma Aldrich or VWR"; the UV kinetics
  section (I.d) refers to "following the previous procedure.2" (ref 2 = Meyer, Tomchick, Klinman, PNAS 2008).

**b) Buffer and pH**
- 0.1 M sodium borate, pH 9.0. Location: Table S7 footnotes a and b (p. S45).
  Quote: "protio-LA substrate in 0.1 M sodium borate, pH 9.0".
- The methods section I.d (UV kinetics) itself does not print the buffer. (I.b: protein dialysed in
  "100 mM sodium borate, pH 9.0".)

**c) Temperatures**
- Table S7 (pp. S44–S45): 45, 40, 35, 30, 25, 20, 15, 10 °C (eight temperatures, 5 °C steps) for WT, S749A,
  S749G, Y317S and Y317F; Y317L has only seven rows: 45–15 °C (no 10 °C row).
- Table S7 tabulates kcat(H), KM-H, kcat(D), KM-D and Dkcat at each temperature, standard error in brackets.
- Methods I.d (p. S4): "at least seven temperatures for each variant studied here".

**d) How ΔEa and Dkcat were obtained**
- Section I.d (pp. S3–S4): kcat, KM, Dkcat "were determined from non-linear fits in Origin 8"; Table 1 / Figure 5 data are
  "mean ± s.e.m from three independent measurements".
- Ea(H) and ΔEa: "determined by linear fits of the corresponding Arrhenius plots"; mean ± s.e.m. "from at least seven
  temperatures".
- Weighting of the fits: not stated. Whether ΔEa comes from separate H and D fits or from a fit of ln KIE: not stated
  (only the definition in Table S3 footnote d: "ΔEa = Ea (D) – Ea (H)").
- Temperature at which kcat and Dkcat of main-text Table 1 stand: not stated in the SI for Table 1.
  Only Table S3 (previously reported mutants, p. S33) has footnote a "Recorded at 30˚C". Table S7 gives the 30 °C
  rows (e.g. WT 323 (7) s-1, Dkcat 56.7 (1.5)), which can be compared with main-text Table 1 (not in the sources).
- Further points printed: Table S7 rate constants are "not corrected for iron content"; I.e gives Fe:SLO 0.8 ± 0.1 for
  the new mutants, "except for Y317S, which had 0.25 ± 0.05 Fe:SLO". WT values differ from earlier reports
  "due to the modified preparation protocol".

  "44% glycerol and 20% sucrose containing 50 mm Tris-HCl, pH 7.5", plus 0.1% Genapol and

## Contradictions and open points found

1. S022 vs Heyes 2009 (T. elongatus): the two hydride ΔΔH‡ values match exactly (8.2 ± 0.6; 0.4 ± 1.0), while the
   25 °C rates, KIE, SIE and the proton-transfer ΔΔH‡ (15.6 ± 3.1 vs 9.8 ± 0.8) differ. S022 gives no source note.
   Exact agreement of value and error suggests reuse of the 2009 hydride fit, but this is an inference, not printed.
2. S051: Table S2 (pH 9) is captioned as hydride/deuteride transfer kH, kD, while figures and Table S9 call the pH 9
   quantity kcat. At 20 °C, pH 9: Table S2 kH 2.46 ± 0.19, KIE 2.46 ± 0.13; Table S7 KIE 2.46 ± 0.24;
   Table S9 "Buffer alone" kcat 3.05 ± 0.12, KIE(kcat) 2.43 ± 0.09. Same condition, different numbers/errors.
   Also pH 7, 5 °C: Table S1 kH 267.6 ± 14.4 vs Table S6 (MTEN buffer, pH 7) 195.4 ± 47.1.
3. S051 Table S3: the fit range "above 15 ºC" applies only to EcDHFR entries; no range for MpDHFR.
4. S057: Table S7 values are "not corrected for iron content"; Y317S has only 0.25 Fe per SLO. Whether main-text
   Table 1 is corrected cannot be seen from the SI. Y317L has seven temperatures, the others eight.
5. S059: methods say initial rates were "normalized to the fraction of bound iron"; Table S4 says "Corrected for Fe
   content"; Table S1 has no such note. WT kcat is 297 (10) in Table S1 (Ref. 37) and 297(12) in Table S4 (data "from
   Ref (3)"). The WT redetermined in Table S1 footnote c (Dkcat 57, kcat 359) differs from the tabulated WT row (81, 297).
6. S059 gives no per-temperature kinetic data for I552A and V750A, so Ea and ΔEa cannot be re-fitted from the SI.
