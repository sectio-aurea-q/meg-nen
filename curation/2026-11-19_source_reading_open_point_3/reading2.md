## Q5. S033 — Kockler 2026, Protein Sci. — deuterated substrate in the Arrhenius / ΔEa analysis

(In the source the soybean enzyme is called GmLOX1 = SLO-1.)

Every place that names the deuterated substrate:

1. **Results, KIE paragraph** — both substrates were used in the study:
   "per‐deuterated substrate (D31‐LA) and a synthetic di‐deuterated (11,11‐D2‐LA) substrate (Figure S2a)".
   Same paragraph: "Km of D31‐LA for the AtLOX1 reaction was found to be ca. 20 μM (Figure S2c)" and
   "(11,11‐D2‐LA) resulted in nearly identical kinetic properties to the perdeuterated substrate".
2. **Table 2 footnote b** (table "Kinetic parameters of LOXs and their reactions with LA"; the D rows carry Ea(D):
   GmLOX1 3.1 ± 0.3, AtLOX1 6.9 ± 0.1 kcal/mol): "D represents 11,11‐D2‐LA."
3. **Table 2 footnote c**: "Km for the deuterated substrate (D2‐LA) approaches or exceeds the critical micelle".
4. **Figure 3 legend** (the Arrhenius plots themselves, GmLOX1 and AtLOX1):
   "natural abundance (H‐LA) and perdeuterated (D31‐LA) substrates."
5. **Results, ΔEa paragraph** — substrate not specified: "reactions with the deuterated substrate"; ΔEa = 0.9 ± 0.5
   (GmLOX1, "Figure 3a") and −0.3 ± 0.6 kcal/mol (AtLOX1, "Figure 3b"); later only "D‐LA".
6. **Methods 5.1 (Materials)**: "perdeuterated substrate (D31LA) was obtained as the methyl ester"; "The 11,11‐D2‐LA
   substrate was synthesized (Offenbacher et al., 2016)."
7. **Methods 5.3 (Steady-state kinetics)**: "deuterium‐labeled (D31 or 11,11‐D2) LA substrates" — does not say which
   one was used for the temperature series (10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40 °C).
8. **Supporting-information caption, Figure S2c** (25 °C saturation plots only, AtLOX1):
   "deuterium‐labeled substrates (D2LA: 11,11‐D2‐LA; and D31LA)".
9. Discussion: only "deuterated substrates" / "D‐LA", unspecified.

**Do they agree? No — the source contradicts itself.**
- Figure 3 legend: the Arrhenius plots (from which the text takes ΔEa) are with **D31‐LA (perdeuterated)**.
- Table 2 footnote b: the D rows, which contain exactly the Ea(D) values that give those ΔEa
  (3.1 − 2.2 = 0.9; 6.9 − 7.2 = −0.3), are **11,11‐D2‐LA**.
- Methods and the ΔEa paragraph do not decide it.
So for the temperature dependence of the KIE of GmLOX1 (SLO-1) and AtLOX1 the substrate is D31‐LA according to the
figure legend and 11,11‐D2‐LA according to the table footnote; the main text does not resolve this.
(Side observation: the Results cite "Figure S2a" for the deuterated substrates, while the S2 caption puts them in
panel c.)

Conditions printed alongside (Table 2 footnotes a, d): 25 °C; GmLOX1 in "100 mM sodium borate, pH 9.0", the other
LOXs in "50 mM HEPES, 150 mM NaCl, pH 7.0". Temperature range "10–40°C" (Results).

MpDHFR fit is printed (footnote b "Above 25 °C" belongs to the TmDHFR column only). The source points to the SI:
"tabulated data ... for the pH and temperature dependences of kH and KIE(kH); activation energies".
(Only indirect hint: the value at 2 °C was "estimated by linear extrapolation from the Arrhenius plots", i.e. 2 °C is
not presented as a measured point.)

**b) KIE at low temperature (below about 5 °C):**
Location: Results, first paragraph. Quote: "a similar fall in the KIE was observed below 5 °C".
Continues: "to 1.98 ± 0.13 at 1 °C (not shown)".
Context matters: the sentence directly follows the **steady-state, pH 9** KIE ("Here, the KIE was almost temperature
independent with ΔEa of 1.5 ± 1.1 kJ mol–1") and the EcDHFR sentence "at elevated pH, the KIE falls below 15 °C due to
a partial change in the rate-limiting step". So as printed, the fall below 5 °C is reported in the pH 9 steady-state
context (KIE on kcat), not explicitly for the single-turnover hydride transfer at pH 7; the sentence itself does not
name the pH or the method, and the data are "not shown". For the pH 7 single-turnover KIE the main text says nothing
about behaviour below 5 °C.

**c) pH:**
- Hydride transfer (single turnover, stopped flow): pH 7.
  Methods quote: "100 mM potassium phosphate (pH 7.0) containing 100 mM NaCl and 10 mM β-mercaptoethanol".
  Table 1 title: "Hydride Transfer Catalyzed by MpDHFR, EcDHFR and TmDHFR at pH 7".
